The place others come to for a holiday.

DTC Resort is rising next to the Dodig Tennis Center in Medjugorje. One site brings together the apartment building, a 25-metre pool, a lagoon for children, a poolside bar, an indoor tennis and padel arena and outdoor padel courts. Apartment owners have all of it a few steps from their own front door.
